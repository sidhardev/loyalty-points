## Long description

Regularhood is a free loyalty stamp card for small local shops in India: cafés, salons, bakeries, kirana stores, gyms and sweet shops.

The shop sets one simple rule, for example "every 5th visit, a free gift". At the counter, type the customer's phone number and tap Add visit. When the reward is due, the app shows it.

Customers don't need an app, a QR code or points. Their card opens from a WhatsApp link in any browser, in English or Hindi.

Free to start: unlimited customers and visits, WhatsApp card and reminder messages, a counter poster, a WhatsApp status image, spreadsheet export and daily backups.
