# Loyalty program checklist for small shops in India

A one-page checklist for cafes, salons, bakeries, kirana stores, gyms and sweet shops that want more repeat customers.

## 1. Pick one simple rule
- Every N visits, one reward (5 or 6 visits works well).
- Reward visits, not spending, so small buyers count too.
- Choose a reward you sell often and that costs you little: a small free gift or service.

## 2. Track visits honestly
- Paper cards get lost and are easy to fake.
- Track visits on the customer's phone number instead.

## 3. Tell every customer
- One line at billing: "You've got your first stamp, 4 more and the next one's free."
- Put a small poster on the counter.
- Post your rule on your WhatsApp status.

## 4. Show progress
- Customers come back sooner when they know they're one or two visits away.
- Send them their card on WhatsApp after each visit.

## 5. Win back the ones who stop coming
- Check who hasn't visited for a few weeks.
- Send one short, friendly "we miss you" message, and mention their reward if they're close.
