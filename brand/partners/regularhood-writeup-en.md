Regularhood: a loyalty card for your shop, on WhatsApp

Every shop has regulars. Regularhood helps you thank them, so they keep choosing your shop over the one next door.

How it works
1. You set one simple rule, for example: "Every 5 visits, a free coffee" or "Every 6 haircuts, one free". You pick the number and the gift.
2. When a customer pays, ask for their phone number and tap "Add visit". That's it, one tap.
3. The customer gets their stamp card on WhatsApp. They can see how many visits are left for their gift.
4. When the card is full, the app tells you: give the gift now. The card then starts again.

Why customers like it
- No app to download, no sign-up, no QR code to scan.
- No paper card to lose. Their card is always on their phone.
- They just tell their phone number at the counter.

Why shop owners like it
- Setup takes about one minute on your phone.
- See all your customers in one list: who came today, who has a gift waiting, and who hasn't come back in a while.
- Remind customers you haven't seen in some time with a ready WhatsApp message, in one tap.
- Print a counter poster so every customer knows about your card.
- Works on any phone. You can add it to your home screen like an app.

Good for
Cafes, bakeries, restaurants, salons and beauty parlours, ladies' stores, kirana shops, and any shop where people come back again and again.
