# Regularhood — launch video script (stamp card version)

Replaces the old points-system video. Vertical 9:16 reel, ~40 seconds, Hinglish voiceover, Hindi/English text on screen.
Shoot it at a real café counter: one phone, one customer, one shopkeeper.

## What changed from the old video
- No points, no QR scan, no customer PIN: the card is just the phone number, one keypad and one **Add visit** button.
- One rule per shop (every N visits → reward, e.g. a free cappuccino); the screen turns orange on the reward visit.
- Show the WhatsApp card and the "haven't come in 30 days" win-back list.
